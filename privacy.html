Clipboard Library · Android

Privacy Policy.

Clipboard Library is designed by Telenian to help you save, organize, and copy reusable text while keeping your library on your device.

Effective date: November 18, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Telenian ("we", "us") handles information in the Clipboard Library Android application (the "App").

2. Your saved content stays on your device

Snippets, descriptions, folder names, folder customization, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Clipboard Library does not require you to create an account, and Telenian does not operate a server that receives or stores your snippet library.

When you tap a snippet to copy it, the App places its text on the Android system clipboard. Other apps or system features may be able to access clipboard contents according to Android's permissions and behavior. Avoid saving passwords, payment credentials, or other highly sensitive secrets as ordinary snippets.

3. Manual backups and exports

The App supports manual JSON export and import. Exported files may contain all the snippets and folder details you choose to include. You control where those files are saved and with whom they are shared. If you choose a cloud storage provider through your device's file picker, that provider's privacy practices apply to the file you place there.

Clipboard Library does not provide Telenian-operated cloud synchronization or automatic account-based backup.

4. Advertising and third-party data processing

The App may display one advertisement on its home screen using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. While ads are active, Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as advertising identifiers (when available), device and app information, IP address or approximate location inferred from it, ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

Telenian does not intentionally provide the text of your snippets, descriptions, or folder names to AdMob for ad targeting. Advertising data processing is governed in part by Google's policies and your applicable privacy choices.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

5. Your choices and consent

Where required by law, the App requests applicable advertising consent before requesting advertisements. Where supported or required, you can revisit advertising privacy choices using the App's settings. If an advertisement is unavailable or not permitted, the App's core offline functions remain available.

You may also manage advertising-related options using Android and Google settings, subject to the options available in your region and on your device.

6. Retention and deletion

Your locally stored snippets and folders remain on your device until you edit or delete them or remove the App's local data. Uninstalling the App generally removes its private local data. JSON files you previously exported are separate copies and must be deleted from their storage locations separately.

Telenian cannot access or remotely erase a snippet library stored only on your device. Google and other third parties may retain advertising-related information under their own policies.

7. Security

The App relies on Android's app storage protections for its local database. No method of storing data on a device is completely risk-free. Protect your device with a screen lock and keep exported JSON backups in a secure location, particularly if they contain personal information.

8. Children's privacy

Clipboard Library is a general-purpose productivity utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not intentionally request that users provide personal information to Telenian through the App.

9. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the effective date.
